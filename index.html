What this is

We opened Ink AI like a first-time customer, then rebuilt the moments that leave money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we see what moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at PreInk: walked every screen from “Welcome to Ink AI” to the first try-on, read your App Store listing and reface.ai/preink, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what people see before they pay.

Your product does something few apps can: it shows the customer their own tattoo in seconds. Today the paywall arrives before they have typed a single word. So this is five ideas, not fifteen, and most of them move things you already own to a better moment. Each one is built into a clickable prototype. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app
